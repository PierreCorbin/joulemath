About

joulemath is a small set of calculators about one question: what is a kilowatt-hour worth when you turn it into compute? Right now that means Bitcoin mining and renting GPUs to AI workloads.

Rules we stick to: every number on the site is either your input, live from a public API, or linked to where it came from. If we can't source something, we leave it blank and say so.

Live data comes from the free mempool.space API (difficulty, block rewards, BTC price) with CoinGecko as a price fallback. It all runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Privacy

If you sign up for the weekly email, we store only your email address and the time you signed up, so we can send it. We do not sell or share it, and you can reply to any of the emails to have it deleted.

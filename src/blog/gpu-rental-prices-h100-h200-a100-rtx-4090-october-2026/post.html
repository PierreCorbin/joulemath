Where these prices come from

  • Vast.ai is a marketplace, so hosts set prices and they move all day. We queried Vast's public offer search (console.vast.ai/api/v0/bundles) at about 20:13 CEST on October 21, 2026 for rentable, on-demand offers with exactly one GPU of each model, and took the lowest, median and highest total price per hour. You can browse the same market on Vast.ai (affiliate link).
  • RunPod publishes fixed on-demand prices for Community Cloud and Secure Cloud on its pricing page, marked "Updated October 8, 2026" and accessed October 21, 2026.
  • Lambda publishes per-GPU hourly prices on its pricing page, accessed October 21, 2026. Prices are "plus applicable sales tax/VAT/GST" and vary with how many GPUs are in the instance.

What jumps out

  • Single H100s are around $2 to $4 an hour. The cheapest single H100 SXM we saw was $2.14 on Vast. RunPod Community is $2.69, Lambda's single-GPU instance is $4.29.
  • H200 costs roughly 1.5x to 2x an H100 at the same provider. RunPod Community lists $3.59 vs $2.69, Secure $5.29 vs $3.99.
  • A100s are cheap now. The Vast median for a single A100 SXM4 was $0.80/hr, under a third of a single H100.
  • RTX 4090 spreads widely. $0.15 to $2.67 on Vast, with a $0.55 median. The high end is likely a host with unusual specs or bandwidth, so the median is the more useful figure.
  • Marketplace vs fixed price. Vast's medians sit below RunPod's Community prices for H100 SXM and A100, but above for RTX 4090. With samples this small, do not read too much into the H100 gap.

Per kW: what a host could gross

This is the joulemath angle. If you have power and are thinking about GPUs instead of ASICs, what does a kilowatt of rented GPU bring in?

Two assumptions, both ours, not sourced: about 1,000 W per data center GPU (H100, H200, A100) including its share of the server, and about 600 W per RTX 4090 including its share of the host machine. For reference, NVIDIA lists the H100 SXM at up to 700 W max TDP. We also assume the GPU is rented 70% of the hours. Change all three in the calculator.

Compare that to Bitcoin mining at the same moment. Using the mempool.space API (BTC $84,655, difficulty 140.58 T, 3.1409 BTC average reward per block over the last 144 blocks, accessed 20:13 CEST), hashprice is about $0.0380 per TH/s per day. A kilowatt of Antminer S21 XP at Bitmain's listed 13.5 J/TH grosses about $2.82 a day before pool fees and power.

Before anyone quits mining over that gap, remember:

  • These are renter prices. The host gets less after the platform's cut. We have not applied a figure for that because we do not have a sourced one.
  • A GPU kilowatt costs far more hardware than an ASIC kilowatt, and it has to pay that back before the comparison means much.
  • 70% utilization is a guess. Miners earn every hour the power is on. GPUs only earn when rented.
  • Small Vast samples for H100 and H200 mean these per-kW figures can move a lot week to week.

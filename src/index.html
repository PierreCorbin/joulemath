Why miners hold

Many miners keep the BTC instead of selling it, betting the price rises. History has been kind to that bet: in CoinMetrics' daily BTC price data (July 2010 to May 2026), every one of the 4,328 four-year (1,461-day) holding windows ended in profit. The worst was +32% (bought April 13, 2021 at $63,446, sold April 13, 2025 at $83,569). The median was +1,359%, or +482% for windows starting in 2015 or later. Past performance is not a guarantee. Halvings cut the BTC mined, and a falling price hurts holders more than sellers. Data and method

GPUs: we found no cited data on used H100 resale prices, so GPU hardware is written down to zero over its life. Any resale value is upside.

Who can actually do this

  • GPU rental: a data-center grade site, fiber, serious cooling, and customers who expect uptime SLAs. You only earn when someone books.
  • Mining: power and ventilation. No customers to find. The network pays a reward every block (about every 10 minutes), and you can switch off when power gets pricey.

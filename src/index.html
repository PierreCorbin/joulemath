Taxes (optional)
Depreciation

Hardware is depreciated much the same way for ASICs and GPUs: straight-line over its life, or in the US possibly expensed up front with Section 179 or bonus depreciation (IRS Pub 946). Mined coins are income at their market value when you receive them, and selling them later can be a capital gain or loss (IRS Notice 2014-21, IRS virtual currency FAQ). GPU rental is ordinary business income. UK: see HMRC Cryptoassets Manual. Sources accessed Oct 21, 2026. Not tax advice. Rules vary by country; check with an adviser.

Same operation size, per day

Mining revenue uses live hashprice: network difficulty plus the last 144 blocks' average reward (subsidy + fees), converted with the live BTC price. The GPU side is sized to the same facility kW: GPU count = IT kW ÷ power per GPU, rounded down. Both sides include hardware cost (bought and amortized, or rented). Power per GPU, utilization and useful life are our assumptions; change them to match your build.

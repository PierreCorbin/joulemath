Utilization is the whole game

  • You only earn on Vast while someone rents the card. Vast does not publish occupancy; hosts see it only after they list a machine.
  • Salad's number already counts idle hours. Its 4090s had a job running 98% of switched-on hours that week. The 3090 was at 95%.
  • An idle PC still draws power. At 25% rented, a 4090 on Vast barely beats a 3090.
  • Rates move week to week. Treat any number here as a snapshot.

Risks

  • Power price. At EU household prices a 3090 loses money. Breakeven for a 3090 on Salad is about $0.16/kWh with our watts.
  • Lock-in. On Vast, a rental's price and end date are fixed. You must keep the machine up until every contract ends.
  • Reliability score. Vast scores uptime and connection stability. Below 90% you lose verification, and Vast says the score affects what you earn.
  • Network. Vast needs a public IP and open ports. Machines behind CGNAT can't host. Salad's earnings include sharing your internet connection.
  • Wear and heat. Running a card near full load all day ages fans and heats the room. Not in our numbers.
  • Tax. This is income. TensorDock, for example, files 1099s for US hosts over $600 a year.

FAQ

What does renting out your GPU mean?

You let a platform run other people's AI or compute jobs on your graphics card and get paid per hour. Salad runs jobs on your Windows PC when it sits idle. Vast.ai lists a dedicated Linux machine that renters connect to directly.

How much can you make renting out an RTX 4090?

About $209 a month gross on Salad at its $0.29 per hour average for Oct 20 to 26, 2026. After power for a 600 W PC that is about $130 at the US average price and about $72 at the EU average.

Is renting out an RTX 3090 worth it?

Barely. Salad's 3090 average was $0.082 per hour, about $59 a month, which is less than the power for a 500 W PC at US or EU average household prices. On Vast at 50% rented it nets about $23 a month at US power.

Which platform is best for a home GPU?

Salad is the simplest for a normal Windows PC. Vast.ai lets you set your own price but needs a dedicated Ubuntu server with a public IP and fast internet. RunPod Community Cloud is closed to new hosts and TensorDock wants data center hardware.

Can I game while my GPU is rented?

Not at the same time. Salad starts jobs after your PC has been idle (10 minutes by default) and asks you to close games. Vast wants a dedicated machine; gaming or mining on it fails verification.

Does a GPU earn more per kW than Bitcoin mining?

Gross, yes. A 600 W RTX 4090 PC at $0.29 per hour grosses about $11.60 per kW per day. A kW of Antminer S21 XP grossed about $2.65 per day on Oct 27, 2026. The ASIC kW costs far less hardware and earns every hour it is on.

How depreciation, April 2028 halving, and CPI/M2 debasement are calculated

Depreciation: AI GPUs write down linearly over 48 months (CoreWeave 10-K); Bitcoin ASICs write down over 36 months (CleanSpark 10-K). Vast.ai net rate: Host receives ~80% of listed renter price (guide). Halving & difficulty: Block reward halves from 3.125 to 1.5625 BTC at Month +18 (April 2028) alongside monthly difficulty drift. Real purchasing power: Discounted by (1 + r)^(t/12) using CPI-U (3.1%) or US M2 supply growth (7.0%, methodology).

Methodology & accounting notes: 1. Hardware depreciation: AI GPUs depreciate linearly over 48 months (4 years) per CoreWeave FY2025 10-K; Bitcoin ASICs depreciate linearly over 36 months (3 years) per CleanSpark FY2025 10-K. 2. Vast.ai host net payout: Vast.ai takes 0% host fee since June 2024; renter bundle prices are ~25% above host payout (host receives ~80% of listed renter dph_base, see Rent Out Your GPU). 3. Bitcoin difficulty & halving: Forward monthly BTC production applies your selected monthly difficulty drift and halves block subsidy from 3.125 to 1.5625 BTC at Month +18 (April 2028, block 1,050,000). 4. Inflation & monetary debasement: Real purchasing power discounts future cash flows and held-BTC equity by (1 + r)^(t/12) using either US BLS CPI-U (3.1% default) or US Federal Reserve H.6 M2 money supply growth (7.0% long-run CAGR, see methodology).

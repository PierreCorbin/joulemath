Clore host fees (https://docs.clore.ai/for-renters/fee-structure and https://docs.clore.ai/for-hosts/server-settings/how-to-price-your-servers-for-rental): on-demand base fee 10% split 50/50, so the host pays 5%; an extra 15% hoster fee applies when the renter pays in BTC or USDT/USDC (0% when paid in CLORE; reducible via MFP Lock). So a host keeps ~95% of the listed price when paid in CLORE and ~80% when paid in USD or BTC (our math, assuming the fees stack on the listed price).

## Clore marketplace (public API, read 10:23 CET)
Source: https://api.clore.ai/v1/marketplace (no auth header needed). `price.usd.on_demand_usd` = host's on-demand price in USD per day per server (https://clore.ai/api-docs); marketplace price is the host's base price without fees (https://docs.clore.ai/for-renters/fee-structure). Raw: /workspace/snap/clore_marketplace_2026-11-17.json. Script: /workspace/snap/clore_method.py.
Method: "servers listed" = servers whose gpu_array is all that model; "rented now" = `rented` true at read time; prices from single-GPU servers, not rented, on_demand_usd > 0.

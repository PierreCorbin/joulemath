## Our math used on the pages
Whole 5090 PC = 575 W + 150 W = 725 W while working, 100 W idle (same convention as the rent-out guide). US 18.16 c/kWh (EIA Aug 2026), EU €0.2832 x 1.1188 (Eurostat H1 2026, ECB Oct 26) = $0.3168. 30 days = 720 h.
5090 Salad: $0.3648 x 720 = $263/mo gross; 522 kWh; net ~$168 US / ~$97 EU. Payback at $5,999: ~36 mo US, ~62 mo EU (before resale).
